# AGENTS.md

## Project

AttoMate Landing Page

AttoMate is an AI Automation Agency.

AttoMate is NOT a chatbot company.

The website should sell business outcomes and automation solutions first. AttoMate chatbot is only an entry product.

## Positioning

Wrong:

* AI Chatbot
* SaaS Tool
* Customer Service Bot
* Software Product

Correct:

* AI Automation Agency
* Automation Partner
* Business Process Automation
* Operational Efficiency Partner

Visitors should think:

"These people can help automate my business."

Not:

"They sell a chatbot."

## Audience

Primary:

* UMKM Owners
* Business Owners
* Service Businesses
* Retail
* Distributors

Common pains:

* Repetitive questions
* Missed leads
* Slow response times
* Manual follow-up
* Operational overload

## Design Philosophy

Reference:

* kirim.chat
* Linear
* Arc
* Raycast

Avoid:

* Generic SaaS templates
* Corporate consulting websites
* Enterprise software aesthetics
* AI-generated layouts

The design must feel:

* Warm
* Human
* Playful
* Memorable
* Modern
* Premium

## Visual Rules

Use:

* Pastel tomato palette
* Organic shapes
* Highlights
* Squiggly lines
* Doodle arrows
* Floating badges
* Dotted textures
* Brand motifs

Avoid:

* Glassmorphism
* AI gradients
* Neon effects
* Futuristic visuals
* Robot illustrations
* Chatbot mascots

## Color Palette

Primary: #D9675A

Primary Dark: #C75648

Soft: #FBE7E3

Background: #FFF5F3

Text: #1D1D1D

Muted: #666666

Border: #EAEAEA

## Typography

Heading: Geist

Body: Inter

Large bold headlines.

Readable body text.

Avoid oversized vertical headlines.

## Layout Rules

Do not create:

* Hero left + dashboard right SaaS template
* Endless feature grids
* Large empty whitespace
* Repeated card layouts

Do create:

* Visual rhythm
* Asymmetrical sections
* Storytelling layouts
* Mixed content density
* Unique section compositions

Every section should have a distinct visual idea.

## Hero Rules

Hero must communicate:

Less manual work.
More business growth.

Focus on outcomes.

Not AI features.

Primary CTA:

* Hubungi Kami

Secondary CTA:

* Lihat Demo

Never use:

* Sign In
* Get Started
* Register
* Try Free

## Page Structure

1. Hero

2. Problems We Solve

3. Automation Solutions

4. AttoMate Product

5. How We Work

6. Industries

7. Results / Case Studies

8. FAQ

9. CTA

## Copywriting Rules

Use:

* Simple Bahasa Indonesia
* Conversational language
* Business-focused messaging

Talk about:

* Time saved
* Faster response
* Fewer missed opportunities
* Operational efficiency
* Business growth

Avoid:

* AI buzzwords
* Technical jargon
* Corporate language
* Feature dumping

## Decorative Rules

Decorations must guide attention.

Never decorative for decoration's sake.

Use:

* Highlights
* Underlines
* Arrows
* Scribbles
* Badges

to support hierarchy.

## Quality Check

Reject any design that feels like:

* HubSpot
* Salesforce
* SAP
* Generic SaaS template
* AI website builder output

Accept designs that feel:

* Human-made
* Brand-driven
* Memorable
* Visually rich
* Modern Indonesian startup quality

## Final Goal

Build a landing page that looks like a premium AI Automation Agency.

The visitor should remember the brand, understand the business value within seconds, and feel encouraged to start a conversation.

The website should never feel like a generic chatbot SaaS product.
